The rule of twelfths

The water does not rise at a steady rate. Split the rise into six hours:

  1. Hours 1 and 6 move one twelfth each.
  2. Hours 2 and 5 move two twelfths each.
  3. Hours 3 and 4 move three twelfths each.

Your pages stay yours

  • Some signed-in pages are read through that site's own API, with the session you already have

Questions

Chrome asked for access to the site. Is that expected?

Yes. The extension has no standing access to most sites. If Chrome shows "Wants access to this site", click Allow, then click the icon again.

Why not give the model the URL?

Many tools cannot open it, and a page behind your login is out of reach for them. The copy is what you see, with your session.

Part of a long conversation is missing. Why?

Many apps keep only recent messages in the page. Scroll up to load the older ones, then copy again. Copies of AI chats say when older messages were not loaded. Other chat apps do not say it.

The userscript button is in the way. Can I move it?

Drag it. The position is saved for each site.

My site already has a copy button. How do I turn yours off?

Give any element the ID copy_as_markdown_btn and the page button stays hidden. The toolbar icon still works. This page does that.
